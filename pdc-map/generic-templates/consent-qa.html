Q&A: Consent to Share and Use Information with the Philanthropy Data Commons
Legal Note

This Q&A is not a legal document and does not create any legal obligations for you or for the Foundation. It seeks to provide helpful information about the PDC and how your data may be used if you consent to its use.

What is the Philanthropy Data Commons (PDC)?

The PDC is a sector-wide data infrastructure project. It is currently managed by Mac Services, Inc., an affiliate of the John D. and Catherine T. MacArthur Foundation, with the expectation that over time it will be managed by a new or existing tax-exempt organization that aligns with the purposes and values of the PDC. It is intended to assist funders, nonprofits and philanthropy-serving organizations in reducing repetitive reporting, improving discoverability, and making it easier to work together toward shared goals using common data. Participation is voluntary.

What am I consenting to?

By consenting, you are agreeing that the Foundation may share the information you submitted with the PDC and its participating organizations to support sector-wide tools, platforms, and initiatives. This includes sharing data with other PDC participants who have signed the Data Use Agreement (DUA), as described in more detail below.

Who else will see or use our information?

The Foundation will only share your data with organizations that have signed the PDC's DUA and have agreed to a common set of guiding principles concerning their use of data, including obligations to only use it in furtherance of charitable purposes and to protect and keep it confidential where appropriate. For more information about the DUA, please review this preview document.

What kind of information is shared?

Generally, the PDC will receive proposals you submit and related organizational data—this could include your mission, budget size, focus areas, or project descriptions. However, any sensitive data, including demographic information, will not be shared with the PDC.

How is our data protected?

The PDC and participating organizations use reasonable safeguards to protect your data from unauthorized access, in line with the terms of the DUA.
